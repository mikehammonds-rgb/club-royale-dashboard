# Club Royale Offer Compass

A mobile-first private dashboard for Club Royale casino offers and eligible Royal Caribbean sailings.

## What it includes

- Overview with the most time-sensitive offer and account totals
- Complete offer library: 16 usable offer slots across 12 unique codes (verified October 26, 2026, for Mike)

- Cabin-first best-offer ranking, with Free Play used only after cabin category
- Expandable eligible-offer comparisons without horizontal tables
